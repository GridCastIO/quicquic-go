## Whole-pipeline map and current design

Application `SendStream.Write` supplies stream content; the connection goroutine packs frames, owns recovery and controls BBR pacing. On an unregistered endpoint packet construction registers normal recovery and the per-connection send queue owns the asynchronous socket write. A granted endpoint installs `nativeOrdinarySender` even for a connection without multipath negotiation. Construction creates local completion identities and transfers payload/protection work to the bounded endpoint writer; the connection keeps completion tokens and packet metadata. The endpoint claims a request, sets its deadline, protects/submits UDP/GSO, clears the deadline and publishes an immutable result. The connection consumes that result before receives/timers, qualifies delivery and ECN evidence, and returns credit after payload disposal. A peer ACK settles transport delivery; receiver consumption establishes useful output. Close/revocation preserves pending native borrows and exact-once disposal.

The granted path moves encryption to the endpoint writer; this is placement, not removed work. BBR makes at most one owned handoff per emission opportunity, so the endpoint may incur more fixed submission work per useful byte. Its local completion path explicitly declines the existing Reno accepted-span optimization and invokes scalar rich delivery, ECN, loss-feedback and timer work for every packet. Loss scanning already exits when the physical history prefix is above the largest ACK; do not infer quadratic scans. Early ACKs before native completion fence delivery timing and may affect BBR rate estimation separately from CPU cost.

Inspected owners: `send_stream.go`, `packet_emission.go`, `packet_emission_bbr.go`, prepared/local emission, native protection/staging/completion, `connection.go`, and local completion/congestion dispatch in `internal/ackhandler`. Failure and path transitions have source-level coverage here; this investigation has not yet measured their cost or requalified multipath interop, ECN, migration, loss, GSO-off or DATAGRAM paths. No product change retained yet.
